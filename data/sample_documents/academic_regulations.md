# Academic Regulations & Policies (2026-2027)

## 1. Attendance Requirements
1.1 **Minimum Attendance Criteria**: All undergraduate and postgraduate students must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in each registered course (theory and laboratory combined) to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examinations.
1.2 **Condonation of Attendance**: 
   - Attendance between **65% and 74%** may be condoned by the Academic Dean on valid medical grounds or official participation in university-sponsored extra-curricular events.
   - A non-refundable condonation fee of $50 per course applies.
   - Medical certificates must be submitted to the HOD within 3 working days of returning to campus.
1.3 **Shortage of Attendance (<65%)**: 
   - Students with attendance below **65%** under any circumstances will **NOT** be eligible to write the final examination for that course.
   - Such students will be declared "Detained" (Grade 'F-ATT') and must re-register for the course in the next available semester.

## 2. Grading System & Passing Criteria
2.1 **Passing Minimum**: A student must secure a minimum of **40% marks in the Semester End Exam** and an aggregate of **50% overall** (Internal Assessments + Semester End Exam) to pass a course.
2.2 **Letter Grades**:
   - O (Outstanding): 90-100% (10 Grade Points)
   - A+ (Excellent): 80-89% (9 Grade Points)
   - A (Very Good): 70-79% (8 Grade Points)
   - B+ (Good): 60-69% (7 Grade Points)
   - B (Above Average): 50-59% (6 Grade Points)
   - F (Fail): Below 50% (0 Grade Points)
2.3 **Re-examinations & Backlogs**: 
   - Supplementary exams are conducted within 30 days of result publication for students with F grades.
   - Maximum allowed backlog attempts per subject is 4.

## 3. Academic Integrity & Malpractice
3.1 Plagiarism exceeding 15% in assignments or lab reports results in automatic zero grade.
3.2 Carrying unauthorized notes or electronic devices into the exam hall will lead to cancellation of all exams for that semester and a 1-year suspension.
