# Frequently Asked Questions (Student FAQs)

## Q1: How do I request an official academic transcript or bonafide certificate?
**A**: Academic transcripts and bonafide certificates can be requested through the Student Portal under 'Certificates Section' or directly at the Registrar Office (Window 3). Processing time is 3 working days. Fee is $10 per copy.

## Q2: What are the campus library timings during examination weeks?
**A**: During regular semester weeks, the main library is open from 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM. During exam weeks, the library operating hours are extended to 24x7.

## Q3: What is the procedure for hostel leave approval?
**A**: Hostel residents must submit an online Outpass Request via the Hostel App at least 24 hours prior to departure. Warden approval and parent SMS verification are required for overnight leaves.

## Q4: How do I apply for a fee installment or scholarship extension?
**A**: Submit a formal written application addressed to the Finance Officer along with income certificate and previous semester marksheets before the 10th of the semester start month.

## Q5: Whom do I contact if I lose my College Student ID card?
**A**: Report the loss to Security Desk, pay $15 duplicate ID fee at the Cashier counter, and submit receipt to the Admin Block (Room 102) for duplicate card issuance within 48 hours.
