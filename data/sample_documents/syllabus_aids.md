# Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science  
**Branch Code:** AI&DS / AD  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
The B.Tech program in AI & Data Science combines statistics, computer science, data engineering, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to solve complex data-driven problems.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester III (AI & DS)
- **25MAT204**: Mathematical Foundations for Data Science (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
  - Linear Algebra, Matrix Decompositions (SVD, PCA), Multivariate Calculus, Optimization.
- **25AID111**: Essentials of Data Science (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Data Collection, Data Cleaning, EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis), Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Feature Engineering.
- **25CSE201**: Data Structures and Algorithms (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **25CSE202**: Object Oriented Programming (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **HU1010-1**: Research Methodology (HSMC) - 2 Credits (2-0-0)

### Semester IV (AI & DS)
- **25MAT002**: Applied Probability and Random Processes (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **25AID201**: Database Systems & Data Warehousing (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Relational Models, SQL, NoSQL Databases (MongoDB, Cassandra), Data Warehouse Schemas (Star, Snowflake), ETL Pipelines.
- **25AID202**: Foundations of Machine Learning (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Regression (Linear, Logistic), Classification (KNN, Naive Bayes, Decision Trees, Random Forests), Support Vector Machines, Gradient Boosting (XGBoost).
- **25AID203**: Big Data Analytics (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
  - Hadoop Architecture, HDFS, MapReduce, Apache Spark RDDs and DataFrames, PySpark Analytics.

### Semester V (AI & DS)
- **25AID301**: Deep Learning and Neural Networks (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Perceptrons, Backpropagation, CNNs for Computer Vision, RNNs & LSTMs for Sequential Data, Transformers & Attention Mechanisms, PyTorch/TensorFlow.
- **25AID302**: Natural Language Processing (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Text Tokenization, Lemmatization, Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe), N-gram Models, Sentiment Analysis, Large Language Models (LLMs), Prompt Engineering.
- **25AID31x**: Professional Elective I - 3 Credits
  - Options: Computer Vision, Reinforcement Learning, Time Series Forecasting.

### Semester VI (AI & DS)
- **25AID303**: Generative AI & MLOps (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - GANs, Diffusion Models, Model Deployment (Flask/FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes), CI/CD for ML, Model Monitoring, MLflow.
- **25AID304**: AI Ethics, Governance & Privacy (HSMC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **25AID32x**: Professional Elective II - 3 Credits
- **25AID360**: Capstone Mini Project - 2 Credits

### Semester VII (AI & DS)
- **25AID401**: Business Analytics & Decision Support (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **25AID41x**: Professional Elective III - 3 Credits
- **25AID41x**: Professional Elective IV - 3 Credits
- **25AID470**: Major Project Phase I - 4 Credits

### Semester VIII (AI & DS)
- **25AID480**: Major Project Phase II & Dissertation - 8 Credits
- **25AID481**: Industry Internship - 4 Credits
