# Examination Guidelines & Procedures

## 1. Hall Tickets & Exam Entry
1.1 Hall tickets are released 7 days prior to the commencement of examinations on the Student Portal.
1.2 Students must bring a physical printed copy of their Hall Ticket and valid College ID Card. Entry without ID card is strictly prohibited.
1.3 Entry into the examination hall is permitted up to 15 minutes after the scheduled start time. No student will be allowed to enter after 15 minutes.
1.4 Students are not permitted to leave the examination room during the first 60 minutes of the exam.

## 2. Re-evaluation & Answer Script Copy
2.1 **Photocopy Request**: Students can request a digital photocopy of their evaluated answer script within 5 days of result declaration by paying a fee of $20 per script.
2.2 **Re-evaluation Request**: 
   - Applications for re-evaluation must be submitted online within 10 days of result announcement.
   - Re-evaluation fee is $35 per subject.
   - If the revised mark differs by more than 15% from the original mark, a third evaluator assesses the script and the average of the top two marks is awarded.

## 3. Exam Schedule & Timings
3.1 Morning Session: 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM (Doors open at 09:10 AM).
3.2 Afternoon Session: 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM (Doors open at 01:40 PM).
3.3 Seating plans are published on the department notice boards 1 hour before exam commencement.
