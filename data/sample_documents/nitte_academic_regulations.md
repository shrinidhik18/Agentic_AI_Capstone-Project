# NITTE (Deemed to be University) - Academic Regulations & Policies (AY 2026-27)

## 1. Attendance Requirements & Rules
1.1 **Minimum Attendance Criteria**: Every student must put in a minimum attendance of **85%** in each registered course (theory, tutorial, practical/PBL combined) to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE).
1.2 **Condonation of Attendance Shortage**:
   - Attendance shortage between **75% and 84%** (up to 10% condonation) may be condoned by the Principal / Academic Dean on valid medical grounds, participation in University level sports, cultural activities, seminars, workshops, or paper presentations.
   - Medical certificates must be submitted within 3 working days to the HOD.
1.3 **Detention due to Attendance Shortage (<75%)**:
   - Students securing less than **75% attendance** in any course under any circumstances are awarded an **'N' grade** (Grade point = 0) and are **debarred / detained** from writing the SEE for that course.
   - Such students must re-register for the course when it is offered next in a main (Odd/Even) or Summer semester.

## 2. Credit System & Registration Limits
2.1 **Total Credits Required**: Regular 4-Year B.Tech program requires earning a minimum of **165 Credits** (or 164 for Cyber Security). Lateral Entry Diploma students require **123 Credits**.
2.2 **Semester Credit Limits**: 
   - Minimum: **18 credits** per semester.
   - Maximum: **26 credits** per semester (up to 28 credits for students pursuing Honors or Minor degrees).
2.3 **Credit Equivalence**:
   - 1 hour Lecture per week = 1.0 Credit (15 contact hours/semester).
   - 1 hour Tutorial per week = 1.0 Credit (30 contact hours/semester).
   - 2 hours Practical/Lab session per week = 1.0 Credit (30 contact hours/semester).
   - 1 credit Internship / Project = 40-45 hours of work.

## 3. Evaluation System, CIE/SEE Weightage & Passing Criteria
3.1 **Evaluation Weightage**:
   - **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)**: **60 Marks** (60% weightage).
   - **Semester End Examination (SEE)**: **40 Marks** (40% weightage - scaled down from 100/50 marks).
   - Total Course Assessment = 100 Marks.
3.2 **CIE Breakdown (Non-PBL Theory Courses)**:
   - Learning Activities (Quizzes, MCQs, Assignments, Tutorials): 20 Marks.
   - Sum of Two Mid-Semester Examinations (MSE-I & MSE-II): 40 Marks.
3.3 **Qualifying Passing Standards**:
   - Minimum CIE Marks: **≥ 50%** (30 out of 60 marks).
   - Minimum SEE Marks: **≥ 40%** (16 out of 40 marks).
   - Minimum Overall Aggregate (CIE + SEE): **50%** (50 out of 100 marks) to secure a passing grade 'C' or higher.

## 4. Letter Grading System & CGPA Classification
4.1 **Grade Points & Descriptors**:
   - **O (Outstanding)**: 90-100% (10 Grade Points)
   - **A+ (Excellent)**: 80-89% (9 Grade Points)
   - **A (Very Good)**: 70-79% (8 Grade Points)
   - **B+ (Good)**: 60-69% (7 Grade Points)
   - **B (Above Average)**: 55-59% (6 Grade Points)
   - **C (Average)**: 50-54% (5 Grade Points)
   - **F (Fail)**: 0-49% (0 Grade Points)
   - **N (Detained / Attendance Shortage <75% or CIE <50%)**: 0 Grade Points.
   - **W (Withdrawn)**, **I (Incomplete due to illness/calamity)**, **X (Held for makeup)**.
4.2 **Award of Class**:
   - CGPA ≥ 7.00 (≥ 70%): **First Class with Distinction**
   - CGPA ≥ 6.00 (≥ 60%): **First Class**
   - 5.00 ≤ CGPA < 6.00 (50-59%): **Second Class**
   - CGPA < 5.00: Academic Probation / Non-compliance.

## 5. Honors & Minor Degree Credentials
5.1 **B.Tech. (Honours)**:
   - Student must earn **18 additional credits** in major discipline.
   - Eligibility: Minimum **7.5 CGPA** with no active backlog at end of 4th semester. Must maintain CGPA ≥ 7.5.
5.2 **B.Tech. (Minor)**:
   - Student must earn **18 additional credits** in another discipline.
   - Eligibility: Minimum **5.0 CGPA** with no active backlog at end of 3rd semester.
