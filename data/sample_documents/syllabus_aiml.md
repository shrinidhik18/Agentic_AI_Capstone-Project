# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning  
**Branch Code:** AI&ML / AM  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
The B.Tech program in AI & ML focuses on intelligent agent design, cognitive computing, robotics, autonomous systems, deep learning architectures, and statistical machine learning algorithms.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester III (AI & ML)
- **25MAT204**: Discrete Mathematics & Matrix Algebra (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **25AIM101**: Principles of Artificial Intelligence (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Intelligent Agents, State-Space Search (BFS, DFS, A*, Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning), Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP), Knowledge Representation & First-Order Logic.
- **25CSE201**: Data Structures & Algorithms (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **25CSE202**: Python Programming for AI (PCC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)

### Semester IV (AI & ML)
- **25MAT003**: Probability & Stochastic Processes (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **25AIM201**: Machine Learning Algorithms (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Linear/Polynomial Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, Naive Bayes Classifier, Support Vector Machines, KNN, Clustering (K-Means, DBSCAN, Hierarchical).
- **25AIM202**: Knowledge Engineering & Expert Systems (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **25CSE205**: Database Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)

### Semester V (AI & ML)
- **25AIM301**: Deep Learning & Neural Networks (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Artificial Neural Networks, Activation Functions, Loss Optimization (SGD, Adam), Convolutional Networks (AlexNet, ResNet), Recurrent Networks (LSTM, GRU), Attention & Transformers.
- **25AIM302**: Computer Vision & Pattern Recognition (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Image Filtering, Edge Detection, Object Detection (YOLO, R-CNN), Image Segmentation, Face Recognition, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs).
- **25AIM31x**: Professional Elective I - 3 Credits

### Semester VI (AI & ML)
- **25AIM303**: Natural Language Processing & Understanding (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Text Processing, Syntactic Parsing, Word Embeddings, Transformer Models (BERT, GPT), Sequence-to-Sequence Models, Text Generation.
- **25AIM304**: Reinforcement Learning & Autonomous Systems (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
  - MDPs (Markov Decision Processes), Q-Learning, Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Policy Gradient Methods, Actor-Critic Architectures.
- **25AIM360**: AI Mini Project - 2 Credits

### Semester VII & VIII (AI & ML)
- **25AIM401**: MLOps & Model Deployment - 3 Credits
- **25AIM470 / 480**: Major Capstone Project Phase I & II - 12 Credits Total
- **25AIM481**: Industry Internship - 4 Credits
