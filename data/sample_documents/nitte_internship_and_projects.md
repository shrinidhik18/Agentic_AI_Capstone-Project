# NITTE (Deemed to be University) - Internship, Community Engagement & Project Guidelines

## 1. Mandatory Internship Credit Framework
1.1 **Credit Requirements**: Every student must earn a minimum of 16 to 20 credits through Community Engagement, Industry Internship, and Capstone Project Work.
1.2 **Credit Conversion**: 1 Academic Credit is equivalent to **40 to 45 hours** of work.
   - **Community Engagement (Activity-Based Internship)**: 2 Credits (02 weeks / 80-90 hours during 1st/2nd year vacations). Evaluated for 100 Marks CIE in VIII Semester grade card.
   - **Industry / Research Internship**: 4 to 8 Credits (04 to 08 weeks duration conducted during summer vacations after IV and VI semesters).
   - **Capstone Major Project Work**: 10 Credits (3 hours/week in VII semester Phase-1, 16 weeks in VIII semester Phase-2).

## 2. Community Engagement (CEN) & Social Connect
2.1 All 1st year B.Tech students must complete Social Connect & Responsibility (15 hours lecture + 1 week 40-45 hours activity in vacation).
2.2 Submission of **Student's Daily Diary** and **CEN Report** certified by the faculty mentor is mandatory for evaluation.
2.3 Assessment Rubrics: 25 marks Social Connect, 25 marks Presentation, 50 marks Final Report & Viva-Voce (Total 100 CIE marks).

## 3. Industry Internship NOC & Evaluation
3.1 Students must obtain prior approval and No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Training & Placement (T&P) Cell / HOD before commencing internship.
3.2 Assessment Breakdown: 50% Daily Diary evaluation, 25% Presentation skills, 25% Viva-voce examination.
