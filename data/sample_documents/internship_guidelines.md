# College Internship Guidelines & Policies

## 1. Eligibility & Requirements
1.1 Students must have completed their 6th semester without active backlogs to be eligible for full-semester off-campus internships.
1.2 Minimum duration for mandatory summer internship is 6 weeks (minimum 240 working hours).
1.3 Winter internships must be at least 4 weeks in duration.

## 2. No Objection Certificate (NOC) Application Process
2.1 Students must apply for an official NOC through the Training & Placement Cell (T&P) portal at least 14 days prior to the start of the internship.
2.2 Mandatory documents for NOC approval:
   - Offer Letter from the hiring organization on company letterhead stating start date, end date, and stipend details.
   - Parent Permission Consent Form.
   - Academic clearance from Department HOD.
2.3 Internships started without prior NOC approval will NOT be considered for academic credit.

## 3. Evaluation & Academic Credits
3.1 Internship carries **3 Academic Credits**.
3.2 Assessment component breakdown:
   - Mid-Term Industry Supervisor Feedback Form: 25%
   - Final Technical Internship Report: 35%
   - Viva-Voce and Presentation before Department Committee: 40%
3.3 Internship reports must follow IEEE standard formatting and be submitted within 7 days of internship completion.
