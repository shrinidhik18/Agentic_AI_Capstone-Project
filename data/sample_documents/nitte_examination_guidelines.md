# NITTE (Deemed to be University) - Examination Guidelines & Timetable Rules

## 1. Mid-Semester Examinations (MSE) & Semester End Examinations (SEE)
1.1 **Mid-Semester Examination Schedule**: Two Mid-Semester Exams (MSE-I and MSE-II) are conducted during each semester.
   - **MSE-I**: Covers 40-50% of the total syllabus (typically conducted in September for Odd Semester).
   - **MSE-II**: Covers remaining 40-50% of the total syllabus (typically conducted in October/November for Odd Semester).
1.2 **Semester End Examination (SEE)**:
   - Conducted at the end of each semester for 3 Hours duration.
   - Total marks: 100 or 50 marks, scaled down to 40 marks for final evaluation.
   - Passing minimum in SEE is 40% (16 out of 40 marks).

## 2. Exam Hall Entry & Conduct Rules
2.1 **Exam Hall Entry Timing**: Students must be inside the examination hall **20 minutes before the commencement** of the examination. No student will be allowed inside the hall after the exam starts under any circumstances.
2.2 **Required Documents**: Students must carry physical Hall Ticket and official College ID Card.
2.3 **Malpractice & Ragging**: Ragging is strictly banned per Supreme Court order. Any malpractice in exams leads to reporting to Controller of Examinations and cancellation of paper.

## 3. Re-evaluation, Paper Seeing & Make-Up Exams
3.1 **Re-evaluation and Paper Seeing**: Re-evaluation is permitted ONLY for theory papers upon application within the stipulated time and payment of prescribed university fee.
3.2 **Make-Up Examination**:
   - Facility for students who missed SEE for valid reasons (medical illness/family calamity) and awarded 'I' grade, or students with 'X' grade.
   - Conducted during the summer semester as notified in the Academic Calendar.
   - Outstanding 'I' and 'X' grades after make-up exams automatically convert to 'F' if not cleared.

## 4. Grace Marks Policy
4.1 Grace marks up to 1% of the maximum total marks of eligible courses (or 10 marks, whichever is less) can be awarded to failed courses (max 5 marks per course) provided the student passes after awarding grace marks.
