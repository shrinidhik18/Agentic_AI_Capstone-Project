# Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Computer Science & Business Systems  
**Branch Code:** CSBS / CB  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
Designed in partnership with TCS, the CSBS curriculum balances computer science engineering core topics with business management, financial engineering, enterprise systems, and business analytics.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester III (CSBS)
- **CB3601**: Discrete Mathematics & Formal Language (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **CB3602-1**: Data Structures and Algorithms (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3603**: Fundamentals of Economics & Financial Management (HSMC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **CB3604**: Object Oriented Programming using C++ (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)

### Semester IV (CSBS)
- **CB3605**: Operating Systems & Linux Administration (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3606**: Database Management Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3607**: Business Strategy & Enterprise Architecture (HSMC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **CB3608**: Computational Statistics & R Programming (BSC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)

### Semester V (CSBS)
- **CB3609**: Software Engineering & Agile Methodology (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3610**: Computer Networks & Security (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3611**: Marketing Management & Consumer Behavior (HSMC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)

### Semester VI (CSBS)
- **CB3612**: Business Analytics & Intelligence (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **CB3613**: Enterprise Systems & ERP (SAP/Salesforce) (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **CB3660**: CSBS Industry Mini Project - 2 Credits

### Semester VII & VIII (CSBS)
- **CB4701 / CB4801**: Major Project Phase I & II - 12 Credits Total
- **CB4802**: TCS / Corporate Industry Internship - 4 Credits
