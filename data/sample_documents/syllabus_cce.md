# Computer and Communication Engineering (CCE) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Computer & Communication Engineering  
**Branch Code:** CCE / CC  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
The B.Tech program in Computer & Communication Engineering integrates computer engineering with modern telecommunication, signal processing, wireless networks, IoT, and embedded systems.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester III (CCE)
- **25MAT204**: Signals, Systems & Discrete Mathematics (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **CC1001**: Digital Signal Processing (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - DFT, FFT Algorithms, IIR and FIR Filter Design, Audio/Speech Processing, MATLAB Implementations.
- **25CSE201**: Data Structures (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **25ECE101**: Analog & Digital Communication Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)

### Semester IV (CCE)
- **CC2001**: Computer Networks & Protocol Engineering (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - TCP/IP Protocols, Wireless LANs (802.11), Mobile IP, Network Performance Analysis, Network Simulation (NS3 / Wireshark).
- **CC2002**: Embedded Systems & Microcontrollers (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - ARM Cortex Architecture, Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS), Interfacing Sensors & Actuators, I2C/SPI/UART Communication.
- **25CSE204**: Algorithms & Data Structures (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)

### Semester V (CCE)
- **CC3001**: Wireless Communication & Mobile Networks (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Cellular Concepts, 4G LTE, 5G NR Architecture, Small Cells, MIMO Systems, OFDM Modulation.
- **CC3002**: Internet of Things (IoT) & Sensor Networks (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - MQTT, CoAP Protocols, Edge Computing, IoT Cloud Platforms (AWS IoT, ThingsBoard), Sensor Integration.

### Semester VI (CCE)
- **CC3003**: Optical & Microwave Communication (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **CC3004**: Information Theory and Coding (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **CC3601**: CCE Mini Project - 2 Credits

### Semester VII & VIII (CCE)
- **CC4701 / CC4801**: Major Project Phase I & II - 12 Credits Total
- **CC4802**: Industry Internship - 4 Credits
