# Robotics and Artificial Intelligence (RAI) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Robotics & Artificial Intelligence  
**Branch Code:** RAI / RI  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
The B.Tech program in Robotics & AI combines mechatronics, control systems, computer vision, kinematics, autonomous navigation, and artificial intelligence for smart robotic systems.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester III (RAI)
- **25MAT204**: Linear Algebra & Differential Equations for Robotics (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **RI1001**: Kinematics and Dynamics of Robots (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
  - Forward & Inverse Kinematics, Denavit-Hartenberg (DH) Parameters, Jacobian Matrix, Trajectory Planning, Rigid Body Dynamics.
- **25CSE201**: Data Structures and C++ Programming (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **RI1002**: Sensors and Actuators for Robotics (PCC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)

### Semester IV (RAI)
- **RI2001**: Robot Control Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Feedback Control, PID Controllers, State-Space Control, Motion Control of Manipulators, ROS (Robot Operating System) Integration.
- **25AIM201**: Machine Learning for Autonomous Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **RI2002**: Microcontrollers & Embedded Systems in Robotics (PCC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)

### Semester V (RAI)
- **RI3001**: Robot Vision & Image Processing (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Stereo Vision, 3D Point Cloud Processing, Depth Sensors (Kinect/LiDAR), Object Detection & Tracking, OpenCV & ROS.
- **RI3002**: Autonomous Navigation & SLAM (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (EKF-SLAM, FastSLAM), Path Planning (A*, RRT, DWA), Obstacle Avoidance.

### Semester VI (RAI)
- **RI3003**: Human-Robot Interaction & Cobots (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **RI3004**: Industrial Automation & PLC (PCC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)
- **RI3601**: Robotics Mini Project - 2 Credits

### Semester VII & VIII (RAI)
- **RI4701 / RI4801**: Major Capstone Robotics Project Phase I & II - 12 Credits Total
- **RI4802**: Industrial Internship / Robotics Lab Internship - 4 Credits
