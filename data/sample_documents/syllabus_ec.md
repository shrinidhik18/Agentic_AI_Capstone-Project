# Electronics and Communication Engineering (EC / ECE) - Regulations & Syllabus

**Institute:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte (Deemed to be University)  
**Degree:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Electronics & Communication Engineering  
**Branch Code:** EC / ECE  
**Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)  
**Total Credit Requirement:** 160 Credits  

## 1. Branch Overview
The B.Tech program in Electronics & Communication Engineering focuses on analog & digital circuit design, microprocessors, VLSI design, digital signal processing, embedded systems, wireless communication, robotics, and optical networks.

## 2. Semester Scheme of Study

### Semester I & II (EC Cycle)
- **26MAT103 / 25MAT001**: Calculus & Linear Algebra (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **PH1004-1 / 26PHY102**: Semiconductor Physics & Optics (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **EC1001-1 / 26ECE101**: Basic Electronics Engineering (ESC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
  - *Modules:* Diodes, BJT Amplifiers, Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps), Oscillators, Digital Logic Gates, Combinational Circuits.
- **EC1002-2 / 26ECE111**: Applied Digital Logic Design (ESC) - 3 Credits (2-0-2)
  - *Modules:* Boolean Algebra, Karnaugh Maps, Multiplexers, Decoders, Flip-Flops, Counters, Shift Registers, Verilog HDL Simulation.
- **CS1001-2**: Problem Solving through Programming in C (ESC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
- **HU1001-1**: Technical English & Communication (AEC) - 2 Credits (1-0-2)

### Semester III (EC / ECE)
- **25MAT201**: Transform Calculus & Complex Variables (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **25EC201**: Electronic Devices & Circuit Theory (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - MOSFETs, BJT Frequency Response, Feedback Amplifiers, Power Amplifiers, Voltage Regulators.
- **25EC202**: Network Theory & Circuit Analysis (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
  - Mesh & Nodal Analysis, Network Theorems (Thevenin, Norton, Superposition), Two-Port Networks, Transient Response.
- **25EC203**: Signals and Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
  - Continuous & Discrete Time Signals, LTI Systems, Fourier Series, Fourier Transform, Laplace Transform, Z-Transform.
- **25EC204**: Digital System Design using Verilog (PCC-Lab) - 2 Credits (0-0-4)

### Semester IV (EC / ECE)
- **25MAT002**: Probability, Statistics & Random Processes (BSC) - 4 Credits (3-1-0)
- **25EC205**: Analog Communication Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - AM, FM, PM Modulations, Superheterodyne Receivers, Noise Analysis, Pulse Modulation (PAM, PWM, PPM).
- **25EC206**: Microcontrollers & Embedded Controllers (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - 8051 / ARM Cortex Architecture, Instruction Set, Assembly & Embedded C, Timers, Interrupts, Serial Communication, ADC/DAC Interfacing.
- **25EC207**: Linear Integrated Circuits (Op-Amps) (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
  - Op-Amp Applications (Inverting, Non-inverting, Integrator, Differentiator, Active Filters, 555 Timer, PLL).

### Semester V (EC / ECE)
- **25EC301**: Digital Communication Systems (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - Sampling Theorem, PCM, DPCM, Digital Modulation (ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM), Matched Filters, Error Control Coding.
- **25EC302**: Digital Signal Processing (DSP) (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - DFT, FFT (Radix-2 DIT/DIF), FIR Filter Design (Windowing), IIR Filter Design (Bilinear Transformation), DSP Processors.
- **25EC303**: Electromagnetic Field Theory (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-1-0)
  - Vector Calculus, Electrostatics, Magnetostatics, Maxwell's Equations, Electromagnetic Wave Propagation, Transmission Lines.
- **EC2501-1**: Consumer Electronics (PEC) - 3 Credits
- **EC2502-1**: PCB Design & Fabrication (PEC) - 3 Credits

### Semester VI (EC / ECE)
- **25EC304**: VLSI Design & CMOS Technology (PCC) - 4 Credits (3-0-2)
  - CMOS Inverter Fabrication, DC Transfer Characteristics, Layout Design Rules, Combinational & Sequential CMOS Logic, ASIC Design Flow.
- **25EC305**: Antenna & Wave Propagation (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **EC2503-1**: Space Technology & Satellite Communication (PEC) - 3 Credits
- **25EC360**: EC Mini Project / Embedded Hardware Design - 2 Credits

### Semester VII (EC / ECE)
- **25EC401**: Wireless & Mobile Networks (5G/LTE) (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **25EC402**: Optical Fiber Communication (PCC) - 3 Credits (3-0-0)
- **25EC470**: Major Project Phase I - 4 Credits

### Semester VIII (EC / ECE)
- **25EC480**: Major Project Phase II & Dissertation - 8 Credits
- **25EC481**: Industrial Internship / Core ECE Internship - 4 Credits
